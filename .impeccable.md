# Bridge Design Context

## Users
- **Who:** Socially active undergrads at dense residential campuses (Rice University beta). Embedded in tight friend groups, value peer validation, motivated by meaningful relationships.
- **Context:** Checking the app between classes, in dorms, in group settings. 5 minutes/day max. Mobile-only (iOS primary).
- **Job to be done:** Find a real relationship through people who already know you — without the loneliness of swiping alone.

## Brand Personality
**Down-to-earth, warm, community-driven.**

- Voice: Friendly, honest, casual but not sloppy. Like a thoughtful friend giving advice, not a brand talking at you.
- Tone: Encouraging without being pushy. Confident without being cocky. Fun without being childish.
- Emotional goal: The app should feel like a **group hangout that might lead to something real** — warm energy, light excitement, social comfort. Not a clinical tool, not a party, not a luxury experience.

## Aesthetic Direction

### Visual Tone
Somewhere between Duolingo's approachability and Arc's clean confidence. Snapchat's casual social energy is a reference for how interactions should *feel* — quick, light, familiar. The UI should be warm and inviting without being childish, polished without being premium.

### References
- **Duolingo** — friendly, motivating, gamified progress (but Bridge is less cartoonish, more grounded)
- **Arc** — clean layouts, confident typography, smart use of color (but Bridge is warmer and more social)
- **Snapchat** — casual social energy, fast interactions, youthful without being juvenile

### Anti-References (what Bridge must NOT look like)
- **Raya / The League** — elitist, exclusive, VIP-gated, status-obsessed
- **Notion** — too premium, too slick, too "productivity tool"
- **Neon / cyberpunk aesthetics** — no glowing colors, no dark-mode-first gradients
- **Flat corporate** — no generic SaaS dashboards, no cold blue-and-gray enterprise UI
- **Overly playful / cartoonish** — no mascots, no bouncing characters, no crayon illustrations

### Theme
- Light mode only (no dark mode planned)
- Warm off-white background (#FDFAF7) as the foundation — never pure clinical white
- Warm brown shadow palette (#4A3428, #3D2817) for natural depth — never cold gray shadows
- Blue as the single accent color — #2563EB for actions, #437FFF for nav (LOCKED)
- Status colors (success/error/amber) only when communicating state — invisible 90% of the time
- Plus Jakarta Sans throughout (400-800 weights) — friendly, modern, readable

## Design Principles

### 1. Warm over clinical
Every surface, shadow, and color should feel warm and human. Use the warm neutral palette (#FDFAF7 bg, brown shadows) instead of cold whites and grays. The app should feel like a living room, not a waiting room.

### 2. Community is the hero
Bridge's differentiator is that friends drive the matching. Every screen should make the community's involvement visible and celebrated — friend avatars, vote counts, endorsement quotes, karma badges. If a screen could belong to any dating app, it's missing the community layer.

### 3. Light touch, not heavy hand
Interactions should feel quick and effortless (Snapchat energy). Don't over-explain, don't add friction, don't make users feel like they're filling out forms. Haptics, animations, and transitions should be subtle and purposeful — never decorative or distracting.

### 4. Earned delight, not constant noise
Celebrate meaningful moments (first match, successful vote, milestone karma) with real delight — confetti, haptics, warm copy. But don't celebrate everything. Routine actions (sending a message, scrolling a list) should be smooth and quiet. Delight is earned by the user, not sprinkled by the designer.

### 5. Accessible by default
Follow standard iOS Human Interface Guidelines. Minimum 44px touch targets. Adequate contrast ratios. No information conveyed by color alone. The app serves a diverse campus — design for everyone, not just the majority.

## Design System Summary

### Typography
- **Font:** Plus Jakarta Sans (Google Fonts, OFL license)
- **Scale:** 11px (xs) through 40px (7xl), 12 steps
- **Weights:** Regular (400), Medium (500), SemiBold (600), Bold (700), ExtraBold (800)
- **TEXT_STYLES presets (8):** screenTitle, sectionTitle, bodyLg, bodyMd, bodySm, labelLg, labelSm, buttonLg (consolidated from 18)
- **Rule:** Always set `fontFamily` from FONTS constants. The `setDefaultFonts.ts` patch maps `fontWeight` to the correct variant as a fallback, but explicit `fontFamily` is preferred.

### Colors (~25 tokens — "Less Is More")
- **Philosophy:** 80% neutral, 15% blue, 5% status. Color is information, not decoration.
- **Text:** 3 levels — primary (#1E293B), secondary (#64748B), tertiary (#94A3B8)
- **Backgrounds:** 2 — screenBackground (#FDFAF7 warm off-white), card (#FFFFFF)
- **Borders:** 2 — border (#E2E8F0), borderLight (#F0F0F0)
- **Accent:** 1 blue — primary (#2563EB) for emphasis, CTAs, progress, and visual interest
- **Status:** success (#34C759), error (#EF4444), amber (#F59E0B) — only when communicating state
- **Rule:** Blue is the single accent — use it for emphasis and visual hierarchy, not just interactivity
- **Shadows:** Warm brown palette — #4A3428 (sm/md), #3D2817 (lg), #2E1810 (xl/xxl)

### Shadows
- 6 neutral levels: none, sm, md, lg, xl, xxl (warm brown, escalating)
- 6 accent glows: accentBlue, accentGreen, accentRed, accentGold, accentSilver, accentBronze
- Overlay levels: light (0.35), medium (0.50), heavy (0.65)
- Always use SHADOWS constants — never hardcode shadow values

### Spacing & Layout
- Border radii: 4px (xs), 6px (sm), 8px (md), 12px (lg), 16px (xl), 999px (pill)
- Standard horizontal padding: 16-24px
- Card padding: 16-20px
- Touch targets: minimum 44x44px (iOS HIG)

### Text Casing
- **Buttons:** Sentence case with key nouns capitalized (e.g., "Join the Community", "Help Your Friends", "Edit All")
- **Screen titles:** Sentence case ("Your Profile", "Match", "Community")
- **Section headers:** ALL CAPS with letter-spacing for small labels ("WAITING ON YOU", "YOUR CREW")
- **Body/descriptions:** Sentence case, never all caps

### Navigation Headers
Two patterns:
- **Tab screens** (Community, Match, Profile): Title left-aligned at bold 28px, action icons right-aligned with `gap: 4`
- **Stack screens** (Settings, Leaderboard, etc.): Back arrow left, title left-aligned at bold 24px, optional right action

### Font Weight Clarification
- `FONTS.extraBold` (800) is for hero/display text ONLY — welcome screen headline, large stat numbers. Never for screen titles or section headers.
- `FONTS.bold` (700) with explicit `fontWeight: '700'` is the standard for all screen titles to ensure iOS renders the correct weight file.

### Karma Pill Badge
The karma pill (blue bordered pill showing "X pts") appears in UserRow (Community), ProfileScreen, and LeaderboardScreen. All must use the same design language:
- **Colors**: `COLORS.primaryAccent` (#437FFF) for border/text/icon, `rgba(67, 127, 255, 0.08)` background
- **Icon**: `StarIcon` (from Icons.tsx) on Community/Profile, `EvaIcon name="star"` on Leaderboard — both use `COLORS.primaryAccent`
- **Font**: `FONTS.bold` (700) — punchy but not overpowering; extraBold is too heavy at small sizes
- **Border**: `borderWidth: 1.5` on all instances
- **Shadow**: `SHADOWS.accentBlue` on all instances
- **Community size**: paddingH 14, paddingV 10, gap 6, icon 15, font `FONT_SIZES.lg`
- **Profile size (~85%)**: paddingH 12, paddingV 8, gap 5, icon 13, font `FONT_SIZES.sm`
- **Leaderboard**: Responsive sizing (small/medium/large) — see `LeaderboardScreen.components.tsx`
- **ProfileMatchScreen**: Exception — uses white text on photo overlay (frosted glass style), not the standard blue pill

### Section Header Differentiation
- **Action sections** (needs user attention): Blue accent bar (`primaryAccent`) + blue count badge
- **Info sections** (status only): Soft blue accent bar (`primaryDisabled` #93B4FF) + soft blue count badge
- Applied on: Community screen "Waiting on you" (vibrant blue) vs "Your crew" (soft blue)

### Blue Tint Backgrounds
- `primaryLight` (#EFF6FF): Educational cards (How Bridge Works), celebration states
- `primaryTint` (rgba 6%): Impact cards, caught-up footers, subtle warmth
- Standard content cards remain pure white (`COLORS.card`)

### Haptic Patterns
- `lightHaptic()` — icon taps, pull-to-refresh, navigation
- `mediumHaptic()` — significant actions (accept match, swipe threshold)
- `successHaptic()` — celebrations, completed actions (vote, match confirmed)
- `selectionHaptic()` — toggles, chip selection, photo scroll

### Banned Copy
Never use: exclusive, elite, premium, VIP, select, privileged, chosen, high-end, luxury, prestigious, members-only, invite-only, sophisticated, dating (use "matching" or "find your person" instead)
