# Bridge — App Store Listing

## Subtitle (28 chars)
Community-driven dating app

## Description
Where your people help you find your person.

Every day, your community votes on potential matches for each other, and when they agree someone is right for you, you decide whether to connect. No swiping strangers. No endless scrolling. Just one thoughtful suggestion at a time, backed by people who actually know you.

HOW IT WORKS:
1. Build your profile so we understand who you are
2. We surface one curated match suggestion per user each day
3. You vote on suggestions for your friends – they vote for you
4. When your community approves a match, you choose to match or pass

Dating the way it was always meant to work: together.
