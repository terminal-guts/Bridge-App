# Bridge — App Store Listing

## Subtitle (28 chars)
Friends pick your next match

## Description

Bridge is a community coordination app where your friends — not algorithms or strangers — decide who you meet.

There's no browse feed. No swiping through profiles. You never see an introduction unless your community has already voted to approve it.

HOW IT WORKS:
1. Each day, your community gets pairings to vote on — for your friends, not for you to browse
2. Vote Yes, No, or redirect a pairing to a different friend entirely
3. Only introductions that earn community approval ever reach the people in them
4. When a match lands in your inbox, your friends already vouched for it

WHAT MAKES BRIDGE DIFFERENT:
— No discovery feed or profile browsing — ever
— Introductions require a community vote before they reach you
— You can propose a pairing for two of your own friends
— Voting builds Karma — the community rewards people who help others find their person
— Content moderation and user reporting built in from day one

Bridge isn't about volume. It's one thoughtful introduction per day, filtered by the people who know you best. Your inbox is community-curated, not an open feed.

## Review Notes (for App Store reviewer)
Bridge is a community coordination platform, not a traditional dating app. The core mechanic is mandatory community voting: users vote on proposed pairings for their friends, and a pairing only reaches its participants after community approval. There is no profile browse feed, no swipe interface, and no way to discover or contact strangers directly. Users are verified members of a closed campus community. The app includes AI-powered content moderation, a block/report system, and rate limiting on all social actions.
