# Bridge - Community-Driven Dating

**The community finds the fit. We Bridge the gap.**

Bridge is the first-ever community-driven dating experience designed for busy professionals seeking genuine relationships. We’ve replaced the endless swiping of traditional apps with a thoughtful, human-informed matching system that takes just 5 minutes of your day.

### WHY BRIDGE?
Current dating apps are lonely and time-consuming. Bridge brings the "friends-of-friends" magic back to dating by involving the community in the matchmaking process.

### KEY FEATURES:
* **Human-Informed Matching:** Our algorithm generates daily match proposals that are validated by your friends and the Bridge community.
* **Scarcity -> Significance:** No infinite scrolling. You receive high-quality, curated matches that have already passed community review.
* **Ritual, Not Addiction:** Spend 5 minutes daily voting on proposals and reviewing your own curated matches.
* **Anti-Ghosting:** We make ghosting a thing of the past with a 3-day minimum commitment for every match and required feedback when ending a connection.
* **Deep Compatibility:** Answer tiered "Deep Questions" to help our community and algorithm understand who you really are beyond the surface.
* **Safe & High-Quality:** Our strike system and community moderation ensure a respectful and authentic environment.

### HOW IT WORKS:
1. **Algorithm Proposal:** Bridge generates a potential pairing based on deep compatibility.
2. **Community Voting:** Your friends and other users vote on whether the pairing is a good fit.
3. **Mutual Acceptance:** If the proposal passes, both people see each other's full profiles and choose whether to accept the match.
4. **Meaningful Connection:** Start chatting with a 3-day commitment to actually get to know each other.
